# 06: Expand the evidence-backed Fulong Trail Catalog

**What to build:** A season-scoped Fulong catalog covering the known named Trails, with searchable records for all supported identities and map paths only where topology passes the evidence gate.

**Blocked by:** 04: Make evidence and uncertainty visible; 05: Add curated Video Matches.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The catalog states its inventory scope and Season.
- [ ] Planned, built, named, and operating counts are not collapsed into one number.
- [ ] Each Trail has a stable identity and at least one Source Snapshot.
- [ ] Source-reported attributes retain field-level Claim lineage.
- [ ] Each mapped Trail passes the topology evidence gate.
- [ ] Trails that do not pass the gate remain Unlocated and searchable.
- [ ] Curated Video Matches are added where evidence supports them, without becoming a completion requirement.
- [ ] Catalog-wide uniqueness and publication validation pass.
