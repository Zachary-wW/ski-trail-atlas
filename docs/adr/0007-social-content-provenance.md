# Treat social and article content as provenance-bearing candidates

Information discovered through WeChat articles, Xiaohongshu posts, community
videos or other third-party channels enters the system as a provenance-bearing
candidate. The product preserves source URL, publisher, observed time, season,
content scope, permitted-use state and editorial state before showing it in a
Resort Feed.

The product may show a title, source, date, short editor-written summary and
link when those uses are allowed. It does not assume that public visibility
grants permission to copy article text, images, video or platform UI. It does
not promote a social claim into a Trail fact, operating status or directed route
edge without an independent review decision.

This makes daily information useful without allowing automated collection to
silently become a copyright, platform-compliance or factuality risk. The cost is
more metadata and editorial work, but it preserves source traceability,
season-awareness and the ability to retract or stale an item without corrupting
the Trail Catalog.
